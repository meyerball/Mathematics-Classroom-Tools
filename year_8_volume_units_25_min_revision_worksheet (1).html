Part A: Radius vs. Diameter Recall

[ 3 Marks ]
1. Diameter = 14 cm.
Radius (r) = 7 cm
2. Radius = 6 m.
Diameter (d) = 12 m
3. Area = 18 mm², Height = 5 mm.
Volume = 90 mm³

Part B: Calculating Cylinder Volume

[ Round to 2 decimal places • 12 Marks ]
4. Cylinder A: Base Area = 24 cm², Height = 7 cm [2 Marks]
Show working:
Volume = Area × Height = 24 × 7 = 168 cm³
5. Cylinder B: Radius = 4 m, Height = 9 m [3 Marks]
Show working using Volume = π × r² × h:
Volume = π × 4² × 9 = π × 16 × 9 = 452.39 m³
6. Cylinder C (Diameter Alert!): Diameter = 8 cm, Height = 15 cm [4 Marks]
Step 1: Find Radius first (divide diameter by 2) • Step 2: Calculate Volume:
Step 1: Radius = 8 ÷ 2 = 4 cm
Step 2: Volume = π × 4² × 15 = π × 16 × 15 = 753.98 cm³
7. Semi-Cylinder (Half-Cylinder): Radius = 3 mm, Height = 10 mm [3 Marks]
Show working (find full cylinder, then divide by 2):
Full Volume = π × 3² × 10 = 282.74 mm³
Semi-Cylinder = 282.74 ÷ 2 = 141.37 mm³

Lesson 4 Review

Converting Units of Area, Volume & Capacity

Marks:      / 15
Time: 10-12 Mins
⭐ MeyerBot's Pocket Conversion Cheat Sheet:
Liquids (Capacity):
1 L = 1000 mL
1 kL = 1000 L
The Magic Bridge:
1 cm³ = 1 mL
1000 cm³ = 1 L
Direction Rule:
Big → Small: Multiply (×)
Small → Big: Divide (÷)

Part C: Liquid Capacity Conversions

[ 8 Marks ]
8. Convert 3 L to mL (× 1000) [2M]
Working:
3 × 1000 = 3000 mL
9. Convert 6000 mL to L (÷ 1000) [2M]
Working:
6000 ÷ 1000 = 6 L
10. Convert 4.5 kL to L (× 1000) [2M]
Working:
4.5 × 1000 = 4500 L
11. A soda can is 375 cm³. How many mL? [2M]
Working (Hint: 1 cm³ = 1 mL):
Since 1 cm³ = 1 mL, 375 cm³ = 375 mL

Part D: Squared Units & Area Conversions

[ 7 Marks ]
12. Convert 4 cm² to mm² (Remember: area is squared, so × 100) [2 Marks]
Working:
4 × 100 = 400 mm²
13. Convert 5 m² to cm² (Remember: × 10 000) [2 Marks]
Working:
5 × 10 000 = 50 000 cm²
14. A paddock has an area of 2 ha (hectares). Convert to m² (1 ha = 10 000 m²) [3 Marks]
Working:
2 × 10 000 = 20 000 m²
