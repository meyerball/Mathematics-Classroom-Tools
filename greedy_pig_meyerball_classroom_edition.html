Teacher Guide: Greedy Pig (MeyerBall)

Whole-Class Probability, Risk & Expected Value Lesson

🚀 1. How to Connect Your Class in 60 Seconds
  1. Keep this main screen on your front-of-room projector.
  2. Click 📋 Copy Student Link at the top right and share it via Google Classroom, Teams, or write the URL on the whiteboard.
  3. Students open the link on their phone, iPad, or laptop, type their first name, and tap JOIN GAME.
  4. Their names will instantly populate the Live Class Scoreboard on your projector!
🎲 2. The 5-Round Game Loop (M-E-Y-E-R)

The game is played across 5 rounds matching the letters M - E - Y - E - R. At the start of each round, all students start Standing (At Risk).

  • The Teacher Rolls: Click 🎲 ROLL THE DIE! on the projector.
  • Rolls 2, 3, 4, 5, or 6: That number is added to the Round Pot.
  • The Choice (5-Second Pause): Before the next roll, students choose on their devices to either:
    🪑 Bank & Sit Down:

    Locks in the current pot permanently into their round score. They are safe for the rest of this round.

    🧍 Stay Standing:

    Stays in the round hoping the pot gets even higher on the next roll.

  • Roll a 1 (Pig-Out 🐷): The round ends immediately! Everyone who was still standing scores 0 points for that round. Banked students keep their points!
  • Click Next Round ➔ to advance to the next letter. After Round 5 (R), the top 3 champions are crowned on the podium!
🧠 3. Mathematics & Discussion Prompts

Greedy Pig is an engaging way to explore probability, sample spaces, and expected value:

Probability of Rolling 1: 1/6 ≈ 16.7% Independent on every single roll
Expected Value per Safe Roll: +3.33 pts (2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6) / 6 = 20/6

Great Questions to Ask Students:

• "If we just rolled three 5s in a row, is a 1 more likely to happen next?" (Gambler's fallacy / independent events).

• "At what pot value does the risk of losing everything outweigh the expected gain of +3.33 points?" (Theoretical banking threshold is typically around 20 points!).

🛡️ 4. Host Controls & Moderation

• Inappropriate Names: Click the red 🥾 Boot button next to any student's name on the scoreboard. They are instantly kicked back to the sign-in screen to re-enter a sensible name.

• No Devices Today? You can play physically! Have students stand by their chairs and sit down when they want to bank. You can enter names manually with + Add, or use the 🪑 Force Bank All button.
