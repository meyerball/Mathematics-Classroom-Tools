What is a Cylinder & The Volume Formula

✏️ COPY THESE NOTES ONTO THE BLANK LINES ON PAGE 12:

  • What is a cylinder? A 3D solid with circular bases and straight smooth sides (like a drink can!).
  • Base Area: The base is a circle, so:
    Area = π × r²
  • General Volume Rule: Volume = Base Area × Height

Core Formula to Memorise

Volume = π × r² × h

(where r is the radius of the circular base, and h is the height)

Shape 1: Cube with Cylinder on Top

Shape 2: Hollow Pipe / Cylinder

Outer diameter = 2 m (Outer radius R = 1 m). Inner diameter = 108 cm = 1.08 m (Inner radius r = 0.54 m). Length = 4 m.

1. Outer cylinder: π × 1² × 4 = 12.566 m³

2. Inner hole: radius = 108 cm ÷ 2 = 54 cm = 0.54 m. Hole vol = π × 0.54² × 4 = 3.664 m³

3. Subtract: Outer - Inner = ?

Outer Vol=π × 1.0² × 4 = 12.566 m³ Inner Hole=π × 0.54² × 4 = 3.664 m³ Pipe Vol=12.566 - 3.664 =8.90 m³

Key Conversion Facts to Write Down (Page 16)

✏️ COPY THESE CONVERSION FACTS ONTO THE BLANK LINES ON PAGE 16:

💧 Capacity (Liquids):

  • 1 L = 1000 mL
  • 1 kL (kilolitre) = 1000 L
  • 1 ML (megalitre) = 1000 kL

📦 Linking Volume & Capacity:

  • 1 cm³ = 1 mL (A small dice holds 1 mL!)
  • 1000 cm³ = 1 L
  • 1 m³ = 1000 L = 1 kL

⭐ Golden Conversion Rule:

Big unit → Small unit (e.g. L → mL) 👉 MULTIPLY (× 1000)

Small unit → Big unit (e.g. mL → L) 👉 DIVIDE (÷ 1000)
