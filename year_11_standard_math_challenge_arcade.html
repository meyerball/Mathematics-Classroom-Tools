Station 2: Standard Immaculate Grid Highlights

  • Board 1 (Finance): Simple Interest $I = Prn$ (Linear, passes $(0,0)$); Flat Rate Depreciation $S = V_0 - Dn$ (Linear, rate constant); Compound Interest $A = P(1+r)^n$ (Exponential).
  • Board 2 (Shapes): Cylinder / Cone (volume involves $\pi$); Rectangular Prism / Triangular Prism (polygonal faces only, constant cross-section); Sphere (curved surface, non-prismatic).
  • Board 3 (Data): Median (resistant to outliers, central tendency); Mean (uses all raw data values); Interquartile Range $IQR = Q_3 - Q_1$ (measure of spread, resistant to outliers); Box Plot (displays 5-number summary).

Station 3: Vault PINs

Vault Alpha: 8 3 5 2

4 hrs double time = 8 hrs | $60 \times 5\% = 3$ | Median of 2,4,5,8,9 is 5 | $I = 100 \times 0.02 \times 1 = 2$

Vault Beta: 6 4 9 1

Rectangular prism faces = 6 | $IQR = 16 - 12 = 4$ | Square side $36/4 = 9$ | $6 \times P(\text{die}=6) = 1$

Vault Gamma: 7 0 5 3

Scale $1\text{ cm} : 7\text{ km} \rightarrow 7$ | Gradient horizontal line $y=5$ is 0 | Area $\frac{1}{2} \times 5 \times 2 = 5$ | Mode of 3,3,3,5,7 is 3

Vault Delta: 9 2 4 8

$\sqrt{81} = 9$ | 14 days in fortnight $/ 7 = 2$ | Cube vol $2^3=8 \rightarrow 8/2 = 4$ | Gradient of $y=8x-11$ is 8

Station 4: Target Solutions (Examples)

  • Target 100: Cards [10, 8, 5, 3, 2] $\rightarrow (8 + 2) \times 10 = 100$ or $(10 \times 8) + (5 \times 4)$
  • Target 52: Cards [10, 6, 5, 4, 2] $\rightarrow (10 \times 5) + 2 = 52$ or $(6 \times 10) - (4 \times 2) = 52$
  • Target 75: Cards [15, 10, 5, 4, 2] $\rightarrow 15 \times 5 = 75$ or $(10 \times 7) + 5$
  • Target 48: Cards [12, 8, 4, 3, 2] $\rightarrow 12 \times 4 = 48$ or $(8 \times 6)$
