3 Golden Rules of Similar Figures

1

Angles Stay Equal

Corresponding angles in similar shapes are identical. Never multiply or divide angles by the scale factor!

2

Scale Factor (k)

Find k = Target Side ÷ Known Side using two matching sides. Watch out for shapes that are rotated or flipped!

3

Nested Triangles

When one triangle sits inside another, add the segments together to get the full side length of the large triangle.

Quick Recap Before You Start

1. Matching Angles = Equal

Enlarging or shrinking a shape does not change its angles. If an angle is 65° in the small shape, the matching angle is 65° in the big shape.

2. Scale Factor (k)

Match up corresponding sides (check angle symbols!). Divide the side in the shape you want by the matching side you know.

3. Overlapping Shapes

For triangles inside triangles, sketch or separate them! Remember to add the two parts of a long side together.
