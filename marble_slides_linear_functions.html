Year 9 Linear Functions & Domain Guide

1. Gradient / Slope ($m$)

Calculated as $\text{Gradient } m = \frac{\text{Rise}}{\text{Run}} = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$.

  • Positive slope ($m > 0$): Line goes UP from left to right (marbles roll right).
  • Negative slope ($m < 0$): Line goes DOWN from left to right (marbles roll left).
  • Steeper line: Larger $|m|$ magnitude (e.g. $m = -2$ is steeper than $m = -0.5$).

2. Y-Intercept ($c$)

The point where the line crosses the vertical y-axis $(0, c)$. Shifting $c$ moves the line up or down without altering its steepness.

3. Domain Restrictions $\{x\}$

Restricts which horizontal interval of the line is drawn. This creates gaps for marbles to drop through onto lower ramps!

$\{x > 2\}$ : Line starts at $x = 2$ and extends right.
$\{x < 5\}$ : Line stops at $x = 5$.
$\{-3 < x < 4\}$ : Line segment exists strictly between $x = -3$ and $x = 4$.
