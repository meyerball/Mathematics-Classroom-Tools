Arcade Guide & Station Instructions

Lesson Flow (50-Minute Recommended Schedule)

  • 00–10 mins: Station 1: Math Wordle (6 terminology rounds)
  • 10–25 mins: Station 2: The Immaculate Grid (3 function matrix challenges)
  • 25–40 mins: Station 3: Vault Codebreaker (4 mathematical PIN missions)
  • 40–50 mins: Station 4: Target Number Countdown & Reveal Scores!
Station 1: Math Wordle

Green = Correct letter in correct spot. Yellow = Letter is in the math term but different spot. Gray = Letter not in word. Use the syllabus clue for direction!

Station 2: The Immaculate Grid

Like the popular sports grid, each cell requires an equation that fulfills both the Row and Column headers. Click a cell to choose the matching candidate from the pop-up list.

Station 3: Vault Codebreaker

Every vault has 4 separate math problems (Calculus, Trig, Logarithms, Probability). Each answer gives one digit of the vault code.

Station 4: Target Countdown

Apply standard order of operations to reach the target using the allowed cards. You don't have to use all numbers, but you cannot reuse cards.

Station 2: Immaculate Grid Highlights

Each cell requires functions matching both axis criteria:

  • Trig & Origin (0,0): $y = \sin(x)$ or $y = \tan(x)$
  • Trig & Period $\pi$: $y = \tan(x)$ or $y = \sin(2x)$
  • Polynomial & Increasing $x>0$: $y = x^3$ or $y = x^2$
  • Exponential/Log & Increasing: $y = e^x$ or $y = \ln(x)$

Station 3: Vault PINs

Vault Alpha: 1 3 8 2

$f'(1)=1$ | $4\sin(\pi/6)+\tan(\pi/4)=3$ | $\log_2(4x)=5 \Rightarrow x=8$ | $10 \times P(A \cap B)=2$

Vault Beta: 4 7 9 3

$\lim_{x \to 2}(x+2)=4$ | $3^2 - 2 = 7$ | $\sqrt{81}=9$ | $P(odd)=3/6 \Rightarrow 3$

Vault Gamma: 6 2 5 1

$\frac{d}{dx}[3x^2]|_{x=1}=6$ | $\sec(0)+\cos(0)=2$ | $5^x=3125 \Rightarrow 5$ | $\csc(\pi/2)=1$

Vault Delta: 8 0 4 6

$2^3=8$ | $\sin(\pi)=0$ | $\log_3(81)=4$ | $f'(x)=2x+4 \Rightarrow f'(1)=6$

Station 4: Target Solutions (Examples)

  • Target 100: Cards [2, 3, 5, 8, 10] $\rightarrow (10 \times 8) + (5 \times 3) + 5 = 100$ or $(8 + 2) \times 10 = 100$
  • Target 42: Cards [3, 4, 6, 7, 9] $\rightarrow 6 \times 7 = 42$
  • Target 75: Cards [5, 10, 15, 2, 4] $\rightarrow (15 \times 5) = 75$ or $(10 \times 7) + 5$
  • Target 24: Cards [2, 3, 4, 8, 12] $\rightarrow (8 - 2) \times 4 = 24$ or $12 \times 2 = 24$
